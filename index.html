PTP-Transfer – Privacy Policy

Application: PTP-Transfer (package com.ptptransfer.app) · Developer: NovaSys · Last updated: 12 September 2026

Summary

PTP-Transfer does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data with the developer or with third parties. The app has no developer-operated servers, no analytics, no advertising and no crash-reporting services.

What the app does

PTP-Transfer is an enterprise utility that copies photos and videos from removable storage connected to an Android device (USB drives, SD card readers) to destinations chosen by the user or by the organization that manages the device: a NAS or network share folder over SMB, or another USB drive.

Data handled on the device

To perform a transfer the app reads the files in the source folder selected by the user through the Android system file picker (Storage Access Framework) and writes copies of those files to the selected destination. The source storage is treated as read-only. File contents are processed only for the duration of the transfer and are not retained by the app.

Connection settings

In Enterprise Mode, connection settings (share host, folder path, credentials, app login profiles) are provided to the app by the organization's mobile device management (MDM/EMM) system through Android Managed App Configuration. In Personal Mode, the same settings are entered by the user and stored only in the app's private storage on the device. In both cases the settings are used exclusively to connect to the chosen destinations, and the developer has no access to them.

Network communication

The app communicates only with the servers configured by the user or by the organization (SMB destinations on the organization's or the user's own network). No data is sent to the developer or to any third-party service.

Permissions

The app requests the INTERNET permission to reach the configured SMB destinations. Access to removable storage is granted by the user through the Android system picker on a per-folder basis; the app does not request the "all files access" permission.

Children

PTP-Transfer is a business tool intended for adult professional users. It is not directed at children.
